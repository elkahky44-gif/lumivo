Shipping

Last updated November 24, 2026

We ship the Lumivo HY300 to addresses in the United States. Shipping is included in the price.

How long it takes

Your projector ships from our partner warehouse in China. We usually prepare your order within 2–4 days. After it ships, delivery to the US usually takes 7–13 days, so most orders arrive about 9–17 days after you pay.

These are estimates, not guarantees. Customs checks, holidays and carrier delays can add time.

Tracking

Every order ships with tracking. We email you the tracking number once your order leaves the warehouse. Tracking can take a few days to show movement while the parcel is in transit to the US.

Address

We ship to the address you confirm in PayPal at checkout. Please check it carefully. If you spot a mistake, email us right away at adhamelkahky6@gmail.com. We can only change it before the order ships.

A late or missing parcel

If your tracking hasn’t updated for 10 days, or your order hasn’t arrived 30 days after you paid, contact us at adhamelkahky6@gmail.com with your order number (it starts with LUM-). We’ll look into it and arrange a replacement or refund if the parcel is lost.
