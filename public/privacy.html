Privacy policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

This policy explains what information Lumivo collects when you visit or buy from this store, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, shipping address, email address and phone number, as shared by PayPal when you pay.
  • What you ordered and how much you paid.
  • Messages you send us by email.

We never see or store your card or bank details. Payments are handled by PayPal.

How we use it

  • To deliver your order and send you tracking.
  • To answer your questions, returns and refunds.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting duties.

We don’t sell your information, and we don’t send marketing emails unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

Only the services needed to run the store:

  • PayPal, to take your payment.
  • Our fulfillment partner, CJdropshipping, and their carriers, to pack and deliver your order.
  • Netlify, which hosts this website.
  • Resend, which sends our order notification emails.

Cookies

This store doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies. PayPal may set its own cookies when you use the checkout. See PayPal’s privacy statement for details.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed to handle returns, disputes and our legal and accounting obligations.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing adhamelkahky6@gmail.com. Some information we may need to keep for legal reasons, and we’ll tell you if so.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page.
