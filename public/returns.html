Returns and refunds

Last updated November 24, 2026

If you’re not happy with your projector, you have 30 days from the day it’s delivered to ask for a return or refund.

Damaged or not working

If it arrives damaged, faulty or not as described, email adhamelkahky6@gmail.com within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo or short video of the problem. We’ll send a replacement or give you a full refund. You won’t pay for any return shipping.

Changed your mind

You can return an unused projector in its original packaging within 30 days of delivery. Email us first and we’ll send you the return address. Return shipping is paid by you, and we refund the price of the product once the return arrives.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to your original payment method through PayPal. Once we issue it, PayPal usually shows it within a few business days. Card refunds can take longer to appear, depending on your bank.

Before you open a PayPal dispute

Please email us first at adhamelkahky6@gmail.com. Most problems are quicker to fix directly, and we reply within 2 business days.
